# VibeFind — final validation report

**Validation date:** 9 November 2026

This report describes the current final compact-discovery build in this ZIP.

## Data integrity
- `js/data/experiences.js` is byte-identical to the dataset in the supplied Area Explorer source ZIP.
- 258 records: 188 Delhi and 70 Chandigarh Tricity.
- 258 unique IDs; all 258 IDs successfully resolve through `VF.store.get(id)`.
- All 258 venue-card links generated by the card builder point to `experience.html?id=` with their own matching ID.
- Original `tools/data-check.html`: **43 / 43 checks passed**.
- No prices, venue descriptions, ratings, reviews, opening hours, venue photos, coordinates or verified booking links were added.

## Browser-render and interaction checks
The sandbox blocks ordinary local-file and loopback navigation. To test the current package without modifying the app, the seven HTML pages, their linked CSS and their local JavaScript files were embedded in page markup and rendered in headless Chromium with `page.set_content()`. This exercises the current source code, UI and DOM, but it is not a replacement for opening the extracted folder through VS Code Live Server on the submission computer.

- All seven pages initialized in Chromium with no JavaScript page errors.
- All seven pages were checked at **1440, 1280, 768, 390 and 360px**; no horizontal overflow was detected.
- All seven pages have zero missing local CSS, JavaScript, image and local-page references. No duplicate HTML IDs were found in the seven main page files.
- All JavaScript files in `js/` and `tools/` pass `node --check`.
- The homepage loaded 258 records, 65 Delhi areas and 34 specific Tricity areas. Full area indexes remain collapsed until requested.
- The homepage Area Explorer shows four popular areas per city; Delhi and Tricity pages each show five quick-area shortcuts.
- Searching `Rohini` in the expanded Delhi area index returned one matching item.
- Delhi rendered 9 zone controls; Chandigarh Tricity rendered 4 region panels.
- Explore rendered its initial 24 cards. Search, A–Z sorting, grid/list switching and Show more (24 to 48) were exercised.
- Delhi's North West zone selection returned 42 matching places; Mohali & Kharar returned 16.
- Vibe Shuffle opened, accepted Delhi + Sports filters (27 matching records), and generated a detail link from a real result. It is mounted only on Home, Explore, Delhi and Tricity.
- The Vibe Shuffle floating launcher is hidden over the initial hero/ticker, then appears as a compact orbital control after the visitor begins scrolling. Its mobile threshold is later to avoid obscuring the mobile hero.
- Experience Detail rendered `del-104` as Dave & Buster's Delhi, including its source Maps link and 6 related experiences.
- Wishlist save and saved drawer were exercised using a test-only localStorage shim because the sandbox page origin is opaque.
- The custom cursor appeared in a fine-pointer browser context, showed the `VIEW` label over a venue link and had `pointer-events: none`. The venue-card full-surface link overlay sits above the card body; save and Maps controls retain independent pointer hit areas.
- City count-up stats resolve to Delhi 188 places / 65 named areas / 9 zones and Tricity 70 places / 34 named areas / 4 towns after the stats enter view.
- Contact form required-field validation and preparation of a message containing the submitted venue name were exercised. No network submission is performed.
- The homepage area, mood and shortlist sections are consolidated; research-only ideas are no longer shown as if they were listed venues.
